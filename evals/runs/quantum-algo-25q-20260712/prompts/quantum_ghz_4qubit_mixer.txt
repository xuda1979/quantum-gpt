1. Prepares a 4-qubit GHZ state: H on qubit 0, then CNOT(0,1), CNOT(1,2), CNOT(2,3).

     P(GHZ) = <sum P(0000)+P(1111) rounded to 3 decimals>
   Expected: P(GHZ) >= 0.99 (close to 1.000).
Call main() under `if __name__ == "__main__":`.
