   and the float phase = int(bits, 2) / 4:
     phase_bits = <2 chars>
     phase_float = <value rounded to 3 decimals>
   Expected: phase_bits = 01 and phase_float = 0.250 (the nearest 2-bit fraction to 1/3).
Call main() under `if __name__ == "__main__":`.
