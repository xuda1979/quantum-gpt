     g2 = I X Z Z X
     g3 = X I X Z Z
     g4 = Z X I X Z

     Encoded statevector norm = 1.0000
     g1 eigenvalue = +1
     g2 eigenvalue = +1
     g3 eigenvalue = +1
     g4 eigenvalue = +1

   on SparsePauliOp for each stabilizer.
